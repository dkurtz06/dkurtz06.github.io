Gavin goal also adds a shot on target and a goal to the score. Assist also counts as a chance created. Use − 1 under any stat, or Undo, to fix a mistake.

Chance created: Gavin's pass leads straight to a teammate's shot, whether it's saved, blocked or missed. If the shot goes in, tap Assist instead. The pass two touches before the shot doesn't count, and neither does a rebound.

Tackle won: Gavin takes the ball off an opponent and his team keeps it. A tackle that just knocks it out of bounds, or a foul, doesn't count.

Assist: Gavin's pass is the last touch before a teammate scores.

Shot on target: would go in without the keeper or a defender on the line. Off target: wide, over, post or bar, or blocked.
