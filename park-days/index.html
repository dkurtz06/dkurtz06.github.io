        Good to know

        Getting in

        • Epic Universe has its own parking and entrance on Universal Blvd/Destination Pkwy, separate from the CityWalk garages for Studios and Islands. Allow extra time on Nov 23.
        • Rope drop: arrive 45–60 min before opening. The first 90 minutes have the shortest waits of the day.
        • Thanksgiving week (Nov 23–27) is one of the busiest weeks of the year, so expect high crowds.

        Beating the lines

        • Express: check which Epic Universe rides take Express in the Universal app. Express lines are shown separately.
        • Virtual lines: some rides use virtual lines or return times. Check the Universal app at park open.
        • Single rider lines save big time if the group is OK splitting up.
        • Child swap on thrill rides lets parents take turns without waiting twice.

        On the rides

        • Many coasters require loose items in free ride lockers.
        • Keep phones zipped away.

        Food

        • Order ahead with mobile food ordering in the Universal app to skip food lines.
        • Eat lunch before 11:30 AM or after 2 PM.
        • Free ice water at quick-service spots.

        Florida weather

        Outdoor coasters go down first.

        • Rides close for lightning within a few miles and reopen after it passes.
        • Pack ponchos, sunscreen, a battery pack and refillable water bottles.

        If someone gets separated

        • Pick one meeting spot per park.
        • Kids should know your phone number.
        • Guest Services: 407-224-7840
